# Tokyo, 23–29 December 2026

Seven days, home base in Nihonbashi, two minutes from Tokyo Station. Balanced pace: one
cluster a day, one thing worth queueing for, dinner never before 18:00.

> Sample plan, written for the trip-plan docs. Hours, ratings and prices are illustrative
> and were not checked against a live source. Check every one of them before going.

**Booked before arrival:** Shibuya Sky 24 Dec 15:50 · Christmas Eve dinner 24 Dec 19:00 ·
Romancecar 25 Dec 08:00 · teamLab Planets 26 Dec 10:30 · Skytree 27 Dec 16:30.
References are in your email and stay out of this file.

**The year-end trap:** most museums and every public garden in Tokyo shut for 年末年始 from
around 28 December to 3 January, and the ones that don't shut for the year shut on Mondays.
That is what shapes days 2, 6 and 7 below, more than any preference does.

# ⛩️ Tokyo — Thursday 24 December

**Duration:** 08:00 – 21:30
**Home base:** Hotel in Nihonbashi
**Why this order:** Nezu Museum closes for the year after today, and the Shibuya Sky ticket is
timed at 15:50 for a 16:33 sunset. Those two fix the middle and the end, so the day runs north
to south: Meiji Jingu, Omotesando, Aoyama, Shibuya. Dinner is booked because it's Christmas
Eve and in Tokyo that's the busiest restaurant night of the year.

🌅 Sunrise 06:47 · 🌇 Sunset 16:33

---

## ⛩️ 08:45 — Meiji Jingu

Gravel avenue under 100,000 planted trees, and empty at this hour in a way it never is after
ten. Enter at the Harajuku torii, leave by the south gate.

📍 Open sunrise to sunset (06:47–16:20 today) · free
🚇 30 min from the hotel, Chuo and Yamanote lines
⏱️ Suggested time: 75 min

---

## ☕ 10:35 — Koffee Mameya

A counter, a wall of beans, and someone who asks what you usually drink before deciding what
to make. Not a place to sit with a laptop.

📍 4.4⭐ · open 10:00–18:00
🚶 18 min through the shrine grounds and down Jingumae
⏱️ Suggested time: 35 min

---

## 🏛️ 11:35 — Omotesando architecture walk

Ten minutes of one-off buildings: Tod's, Dior, Prada's glass rhomboids, Omotesando Hills.
Free, outdoors, and the reason to walk this street rather than take the train under it.

📍 Free

Kuma-designed galleries of Chinese bronzes and Japanese screens, but the garden behind it is

Open-air roof deck at 229 m. The 15:50 slot puts you up there 40 minutes before sunset, which

🚇 15 min, Ginza line from Omotesando
⏱️ Suggested time: 70 min

## 🔵 17:35 — Ao no Dokutsu, Yoyogi Park

🚶 20 min through Shibuya

Booked in early November. Christmas Eve is a couples' night here and walk-ins don't exist;

📍 Seafood izakaya, Shibuya · ¥¥¥
🚶 20 min

- If the Shibuya Sky forecast turns to rain, the open deck closes and they refund. The indoor floor still opens.

# 🗻 Tokyo — Friday 25 December

**Duration:** 06:55 – 21:00
**Home base:** Hotel in Nihonbashi
**Why this order:** Friday is the clear day in the forecast and Saturday is grey, so the
Fuji-view day goes here. The 08:00 Romancecar and the last ropeway car at 16:15 fix both

🚌 55 min

📍 Ref in email

**Options (pick one):**

- Nothing today has a fallback if the 08:00 train is missed. That's the price of a day trip.

# 🏮 Tokyo — Sunday 27 December

**Duration:** 08:00 – 21:00
**Home base:** Hotel in Nihonbashi
**Why this order:** The day is one straight line west to east — Yanaka, Ueno, Asakusa,
Skytree — so nothing is retraced, and it ends 350 m up for a 16:34 sunset. Tokyo National
Museum would have been the obvious Ueno stop and it's shut for the year from yesterday, so
Toshogu takes the slot instead.

🌅 Sunrise 06:49 · 🌇 Sunset 16:34

---

## 🦊 08:30 — Nezu Shrine

Rows of small vermilion torii up a hillside, an azalea garden that's bare in December, and
almost nobody in it before nine.

📍 Open 06:00–17:00 · free
🚇 30 min from the hotel
⏱️ Suggested time: 40 min

# 🌃 Tokyo — Monday 28 December

**Duration:** 08:30 – 21:30
**Home base:** Hotel in Nihonbashi
**Why this order:** Monday in the last week of December means the museums are shut twice
over — weekly closing day and year end together — so today is built entirely from streets,
a garden that doesn't close on Mondays, and a free observatory. The Tocho's north deck is
closed on fourth Mondays, and today is the fourth Monday, so it's the south deck, and the
south deck is the one that faces Fuji.

🌅 Sunrise 06:49 · 🌇 Sunset 16:35

---

## 🌿 09:00 — Hama-rikyu Gardens

A shogun's duck-hunting garden with seawater ponds that rise and fall with the tide, ringed by
the Shiodome towers. Matcha and a sweet at the Nakajima teahouse over the water.

📍 4.4⭐ · open 09:00–17:00 daily, shut 29 December to 1 January · ¥300
🚇 20 min from the hotel
⏱️ Suggested time: 75 min

---

## 🧥 11:10 — Shimokitazawa

Two square kilometres of second-hand clothes, record shops and coffee, all independent, none
of it closed on a Monday. Wander rather than navigate.

📍 Most shops from 11:00 or 12:00
🚇 45 min, Yamanote and Odakyu
⏱️ Suggested time: 60 min

⏱️ Suggested time: 75 min

## 🍢 17:10 — Omoide Yokocho

Sixty-odd stalls in two alleys of post-war shacks, six seats each, grilling offal and pouring

📍 4.2⭐ · from 17:00 · ¥¥
🚶 15 min
⏱️ Suggested time: 70 min

---

## 🥃 18:45 — Golden Gai

Two hundred bars the size of a bathroom, in six alleys. Many charge a ¥500–1,000 seat fee and
some are members only; the ones with an English sign in the window mean it.

📍 From 18:00 · ¥¥
🚶 8 min
⏱️ Suggested time: 60 min

---

**Notes**
- Shinjuku Gyoen is closed today (Mondays) and from 29 December (year end). It is not on this plan for either reason.
- Golden Gai bars are one drink, one bar, move on. Sitting all night in the first one is the tourist mistake.

# 🧳 Tokyo — Tuesday 29 December
