Mon 28 Dec · Day 6The day everything is shut

Why this orderA Monday in the last week of December means the museums are shut twice over, weekly closing day and year end together. So today is built from streets, one garden that doesn't close on Mondays, and a free observatory. The Tocho's north deck closes on fourth Mondays and today is the fourth Monday, so it's the south deck — which is the one that faces Fuji.
